## 4. Content & Languages

| Action | FR | EN |

## 5. Interactions

### O-03 ActionButtons
- **On tap "Mémoriser"**: Navigate to Memorization Session
- **On tap "Copier"**: Copier texte + toast confirmation
- **On tap "Fermer"**: Close modal
