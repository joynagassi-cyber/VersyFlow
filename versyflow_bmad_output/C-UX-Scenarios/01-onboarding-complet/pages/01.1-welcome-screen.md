## 5. Interactions

### O-02 FeatureCarousel
- **On swipe**: Change slide (horizontal)
- **Auto-advance**: Every 4 seconds
- **Pagination dots**: 3 dots indicating current slide

### O-04 SkipButton
- **On tap**: Navigate to Home (onboarding incomplete)

### O-05 StartButton
- **On tap**: Navigate to Language Picker
- **Delay**: Visible after 3 seconds (logo animation completes)

## 9. Design Notes

- **Carousel**: 3 slides, swipeable, auto-advance 4s
- **Logo animation**: Scale + fade, 400ms spring
- **Delayed CTA**: Boutons apparaissent après 3s
- **Skip option**: Discret, en bas à gauche
- **RTL**: Support complet pour l'arabe
