## 4. Content & Languages

### O-02 MaskChip States
- **Masked**: Background gris, texte caché
- **Revealed**: Background rose, texte visible
- **Partial**: Gradient partiel (en cours)

### O-03 ProgressSlider
- **Range**: 0% à 100%
- **Default**: 70% masqué
- **Increment**: 10% par tap

---

## 5. States & Interactions

### States
- **Preview**: Tous les mots visibles
- **Masking**: Masquage progressif
- **Revealing**: Révélation au tap
- **Complete**: Tous révélés

### Interactions
- Tap word → reveal word
- Swipe slider → adjust mask level
- Double tap → reveal all remaining
- Long press → mark as difficult

### Animation
- Mask: fade out (300ms)
- Reveal: scale up + fade in (200ms)
- Slider: smooth fill
