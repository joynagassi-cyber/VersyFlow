## 5. States & Interactions

### States
- **Empty**: "Aucune notification"
- **Unread**: Badge rouge
- **AllRead**: Badge disparu

### Interactions
- Tap item → navigation action
- Swipe left → options (supprimer, silent)
- Swipe right → marquer comme lu
- Tap header → tout marquer comme lu
- Pull to refresh → nouvelles notifications
